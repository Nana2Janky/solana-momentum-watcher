Know before contacting

A missing field in OpenStreetMap does not prove a business lacks a website, menu, booking link, or social account. Treat each flag as a research lead, then verify using the business's own website or public profiles.

This prototype uses OpenStreetMap data through a public Overpass endpoint. Coverage varies by area, the endpoint can be busy or unavailable, and searches may be limited. No messages are sent automatically. Respect each site's terms and applicable outreach/privacy rules.
